# Persona and wireframe self-review

## Join the queue 

**Works well:** Alex can recognize the item-entry form and the large **Check In** button quickly on both sizes. The phone layout keeps the form and main action prominent, supporting Alex's need to join while away from the desk.

**Problem:** The screen appears to offer only **To Check**, with no clear way to choose **To Ship**. That could send Alex into the wrong queue. “Have items inspected” and “To Check” also describe the same service differently, which may cause hesitation. Ticket status does not apply until after check-in.

**Change:** Show a home for **To Check** and **To Ship** as clearly labeled choices, and use the same service names throughout. Keep the selected service visible before Alex checks in.

## My place

**Works well:** The ticket number, service, people ahead, and current status are visible in both frames. The phone puts the ticket number and queue position where Alex can find them quickly.

**Problem:** The status message says **“Please wait in the lounge,”** but Alex may be in the loading bay or truck cab. It also does not show what to do when Alex's turn arrives, or address the risk of missing a call in a noisy warehouse. On the phone, the status is less prominent than the ticket number.

**Change:** Use a direct instruction such as **“Completed — please claim the items at designated station”** when called.

## Staff dashboard

**Works well:** **Now Serving** makes the current ticket easy to spot, and each queue entry shows its service and item count. The large **Mark as Done** button is easy to reach, including on the phone.

**Problem:** The current ticket also appears in **Up Coming**, which could confuse Jade about queue order. The main action is **Mark as Done**, but Jade also needs to call the next person and handle no-shows. On the phone, the list requires scrolling, so those common actions should remain easy to find.

**Change:** Remove the current ticket from **Up Coming** and make the order unambiguous. Add clear actions to call the next ticket, alongside completing service. Keep those actions large and easy to reach on the phone
